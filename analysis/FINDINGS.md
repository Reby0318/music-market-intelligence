## Table A6 — Predictive power of early chart signals

Top 40 songs debuting 2018–2024 that survived 8+ weeks (n = 1,004). Rank is inverted, so a **negative** correlation is the intuitive direction: better position predicts a longer run.

| Early signal | Correlation with total chart run |
|---|---|
| Debut rank (week 1) | +0.107 |
| Rank at week 2 | +0.016 |
| Rank at week 4 | −0.149 |
| Rank at week 6 | −0.294 |
| **Rank at week 8** | **−0.380** |

### Week-8 Retention Ratio = (rank at week 8) ÷ (best rank in weeks 1–4)

| Quintile | Ratio range | Median total run | Reached 26+ weeks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (holding peak) | 0.07 – 0.74 | 25 wks | **48%** |
| 2 | 0.74 – 1.08 | 25 wks | 45% |
| 3 | 1.08 – 2.00 | 21 wks | 36% |
| 4 | 2.00 – 4.33 | 19 wks | 19% |
| 5 (collapsed) | 4.38 – 70.00 | 18 wks | **17%** |

**A song in the top retention quintile is 2.8× more likely to become a 26-week asset than one in the bottom quintile — measurable eight weeks after release.**
